## Keyword Usage Strategy

Keywords will be incorporated naturally into:

- Page title
- H1
- H2 headings where appropriate
- Introduction
- Body content
- Image alt text where relevant
- Internal links
- SEO metadata

Keyword stuffing will be avoided.

The article will prioritize useful and accurate information
over excessive keyword repetition.

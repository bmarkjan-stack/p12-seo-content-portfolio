# Sample 2 — Keyword Research

## Primary Keyword

best website features for small businesses

## Secondary Keywords

- small business website features
- features of a good business website
- small business website design
- business website features
- mobile-friendly business website
- website contact form
- website call to action
- local SEO
- website security
- website speed
- website analytics

## Keyword Usage Strategy

The primary keyword will be used naturally in:

- Article title
- Introduction
- URL slug
- One relevant heading where appropriate
- SEO title
- Meta description

Secondary keywords will be incorporated naturally
where they improve topical coverage.

Keywords will not be unnecessarily repeated.

## Search Intent

Commercial / Investigational

The reader is researching website features before
deciding what their business website needs.
